Mobile Setup & Troubleshooting

iOS / iPhone

Apple requires you to add this site to your Home Screen before it can send notifications:

  1. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
  2. Select "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Open the app from your Home Screen
  4. Click Enable Notifications

Android Troubleshooting

If notifications are "automatically blocked" or you don't see a prompt:

  1. Tap the Lock/Tune icon in the address bar
  2. Go to Site Settings or Permissions
  3. Find Notifications and set to Allow
  4. Reload the page and click Enable again
